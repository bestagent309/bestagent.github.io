To buy from Taobao through an agent, choose a platform that accepts your goods and provides the purchasing help, warehouse checks, and shipping arrangements you need. Submit the product link and exact options, pay for the purchase, review the warehouse arrival, then arrange international dispatch. The five platforms below provide starting points for different needs; compare their current terms before placing your order.

## Choose an agent around the help your purchase needs

A Taobao purchasing agent buys from sellers on your instructions and connects the purchase with warehouse handling and overseas shipment. You submit the product requirements through the agent rather than managing the seller purchase entirely yourself.

### CFANS: purchase coordination and review before shipping

CFANS is a China cross-border shopping platform supporting Taobao product-link purchasing, order management, warehouse appearance checks and photos, storage, and international parcel arrangements. Eligible arrivals can be considered for consolidation or separate dispatch.

The relevant fit is a purchase needing both buying assistance and a review before it leaves China. Ask for clarification of the selected variant, then examine the available arrival evidence before deciding what to ship. Detail photographs, measurements, and packing requests need their own scope and charge confirmed.

**Consider CFANS for connected purchase and warehouse handling.** Confirm costs, seller-return conditions, storage deadlines, and a suitable destination line. Standard visual QC does not establish authenticity, function, durability, or fit.

### Superbuy: a defined extra warehouse task

Superbuy’s fee guide lists optional detailed photography, re-inspection, and packaging work alongside purchasing and parcel expenses. It is worth investigating when a particular view or check could change your decision.

Specify the detail, angle, or measurement rather than asking for a general quality guarantee. Compare what the service supplies with the same request elsewhere.

**Consider Superbuy for a specific additional-service requirement.** Confirm current availability and charge. A published service menu does not prove superior inspection or a cheaper delivered order.

### Fishgoo: an explained link-to-parcel sequence

The useful starting point is understanding what happens after the product payment and which decisions remain. Use that sequence to ask about your actual order.

**Consider Fishgoo if its workflow fits your purchase.** Check current fees, review options, storage and return conditions, and destination availability. An English tutorial is not proof of faster support or lower complete costs.

### KakoBuy: grouping purchases from different sellers

KakoBuy’s official purchasing description explains buying from different sellers, receiving purchases at its China warehouse, and selecting goods into a shared parcel.

## How to buy from Taobao through your chosen agent

![Six stages from finding a product and confirming options to warehouse review, shipping and delivery.]({{ '/img/how-to-buy-from-taobao-six-buying-steps.png' | relative_url }})

*Follow current provider instructions. Product payment and international shipping are separate stages.*

The following stages take you from a seller link to an overseas parcel. Follow the selected platform’s current order flow; screen labels and available services differ.

### Step 1: Find the product and save its original link

Read the Taobao listing, specifications, available variants, relevant feedback, and any pre-order or customisation conditions. Use translation if needed, but retain the original option text when a translated label is ambiguous. Keep the URL and important specifications rather than relying only on a recommendation image.

### Step 2: Submit the link and confirm the exact purchase

Create an account with the chosen agent and use its supported order route. Compare imported details with the seller listing: colour, size, model, quantity, and the price for that variant. Check whether the option is the full product, an accessory, a bundle, or a deposit.

For clothing, compare seller measurements with a garment that fits. Ask whether an unresolved detail can be confirmed with the seller before purchase. At CFANS, links that cannot be read automatically can be submitted through the DIY route with the item details for confirmation; this does not bypass purchasing or transport restrictions.

### Step 3: Check destination acceptance and pay for the goods

Provide the country and postcode and flag batteries, liquids, powders, fragile items, and unusual dimensions before procurement. Product purchasing acceptance does not establish an eligible overseas line.

Review the item price, Chinese domestic shipping, applicable service charges, and payment or conversion costs. Use the options offered for your actual transaction and clarify how seller price changes are approved.

> An agent-assisted Taobao purchase separates the goods from the international parcel. Confirm what the product payment covers and what remains payable after warehouse arrival. Product payment does not establish that international freight is settled or that the goods have left China.

### Step 4: Review the warehouse arrival and resolve questions

Check the received item against the confirmed variant using the evidence supplied under the chosen service. Request any additional photograph or measurement needed for your decision. Visual QC is not authentication or comprehensive functional testing.

Report a discrepancy with the order identifier and supporting evidence while the goods remain in China. Ask about seller-return or exchange conditions, charges, and deadlines. Warehouse storage and seller-return windows are separate.

**Review the item before approving packaging changes.** Removing labels, discarding packaging, or opening certain goods may affect return eligibility. Agent assistance does not guarantee seller acceptance of a return.

### Step 5: Select the parcel, packaging, and line

Choose eligible purchases to ship and state the original packaging to retain and changes you accept. Combining goods may remove duplicate packaging but does not guarantee lower freight. Check storage deadlines if waiting for another seller.

Ask for packed weight, outer dimensions, service charges, and eligible lines. Volumetric weight is calculated from dimensions under the line’s own formula and charging rules. A seller’s item weight may exclude packing.

CFANS package-service information describes preview packing and weighing before line selection and freight payment. Confirm its scope, charge, and availability if useful; do not assume every platform follows the same preview process.

### Step 6: Pay international charges, track, and inspect delivery

Confirm the address and line, settle required parcel charges, and check how prepaid estimates are reconciled with final charges. Ask which import or clearance expenses are included and which remain payable. Supply accurate declarations; no agent or route label guarantees customs clearance or exemption.

Follow the international parcel rather than confusing it with seller-to-warehouse tracking. A China delivery status may refer to warehouse receipt. After delivery, preserve labels, packaging, photos, and order records for any damage, missing item, or mismatch claim under the relevant terms.

## Questions about buying through a Taobao agent

### Which agent is cheapest?

Obtain current equivalent quotes for the same basket, payment method, checks, packaging, and destination. A buying-fee percentage or coupon alone does not identify the lowest complete cost.

### Do I need my own Taobao account?

Follow the agent’s account and submission requirements. You may still need access to Taobao to browse or obtain a listing. Do not assume the agent account signs you in to Taobao.

### Can I buy directly instead?

Yes, if you can complete the purchase and arrange accepted delivery yourself. This guide focuses on the agent route for purchasing and warehouse assistance, not a universal requirement to use one.

### Does a refund to an agent balance mean the bank has been refunded?

No. Purchase refunds, account credits, and withdrawals to the original payment method are separate processes. Confirm the applicable terms, charges, and current status.
