Better buying decisions, from product link to parcel

Sourcing & Shipping Notes covers the decisions international shoppers face when buying from Chinese marketplaces: choosing a purchasing agent, reviewing warehouse photos, combining orders, and comparing shipping options.

The focus is practical. What does the first payment cover? Which checks happen before international shipping? Does a lower headline fee produce a lower delivered cost? These questions are more useful than a universal ranking.

What we cover

  • Taobao agents: fees, supported buying workflows, QC scope, payment options, and return conditions.
  • China shipping: packed dimensions, chargeable weight, consolidation, route eligibility, and total cost.
  • Related buying questions involving Tmall, 1688, and Weidian.

How comparisons work

“Best Taobao agent” depends on the buyer's destination, product, inspection needs, and budget. “Cheapest China shipping agent” requires current quotes for the same parcel and service requirements. We do not treat a headline commission, coupon, or example rate as proof that a provider wins overall.

Guides distinguish published service information, estimates, and editorial judgment. Where a comparison needs current fees or routes, the relevant conditions and review date should accompany it. Personal tests, order histories, certifications, or professional credentials are only described when they can be substantiated.

Corrections and order support

For source suggestions or editorial corrections, open a repository issue. For orders, payments, or shipments, contact the relevant provider through its official support channel. Do not post private account information or order details here.
