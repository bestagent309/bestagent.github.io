About Sourcing & Shipping Notes

I write about buying from Chinese marketplaces and the practical decisions that follow: checking seller terms, reviewing warehouse photos, choosing packaging, comparing shipping quotes, and understanding the full delivered cost.

My focus is the handoff between sourcing and logistics. A low item price is only one part of an order; quantities, inspection scope, parcel dimensions, return conditions, and destination requirements also shape the outcome.

What you will find here

  • Buying guides for Taobao, Tmall, 1688, and Weidian.
  • Ways to evaluate shopping agents and purchasing workflows.
  • Warehouse checks, sizing questions, consolidation, and packaging decisions.
  • Shipping estimates, chargeable weight, tracking, and delivery troubleshooting.

How I approach a guide

I separate published policies from estimates and editorial judgment. Comparisons should use the same destination, product requirements, and service scope. Prices, routes, and policies can change, so each order needs a current check.

This site does not claim supplier certification, product authentication, or guaranteed customs outcomes. Any sponsorship, affiliate link, or commercial relationship relevant to a recommendation should be disclosed with that content.

For corrections or source suggestions, open an issue in the site repository. Please do not post order numbers, payment information, or private account details.
