Finding the cheapest China shipping agent for your parcel

A useful answer needs a specific parcel. The cheapest eligible option can change with the destination, packed dimensions, weight, contents, and service requirements. Compare current quotes on that basis before choosing a provider.

Give each provider the same information

  • Destination country and postal area, without publishing your private address.
  • Item categories and any characteristics that affect route eligibility.
  • Quantity, estimated or measured packed weight, and outer dimensions.
  • Packaging requirements and whether you want to retain original boxes.
  • Delivery preferences and any optional coverage you require.

Label estimates clearly. A quote based on a guessed weight should not be presented as a final packed price. Ask how the provider resolves a difference after weighing or route confirmation.

Compare more than the headline shipping rate

Record the international charge, applicable handling or packaging fees, payment and conversion costs, route surcharges, and the treatment of destination import charges. When comparing the entire purchase, also include the product and China domestic delivery costs.

Ask how chargeable weight is calculated for the selected route, including any rounding rules. Do not apply one dimensional-weight divisor to every line or assume a listed transit estimate covers purchasing and warehouse time.

Consolidation and packaging

Compare combined and separate parcels where appropriate. Combining orders may reduce duplicate packaging, but the actual result depends on size, weight, contents, and eligible routes. Removing a box or compressing a garment can affect protection and possible return conditions; approve those changes deliberately.

Keep a comparison sheet

For each quote, record the provider, quote date, parcel assumptions, eligible route, included charges, excluded charges, and unresolved questions. An unavailable route or incomplete price is not a winning quote.
